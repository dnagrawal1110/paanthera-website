Bulk & Wholesale

Do you accept bulk or wholesale orders?
Yes — bulk and wholesale orders are our speciality. We work with wholesalers, sports clubs, retailers, and distributors across global markets. Whether you need 200 pieces or 20,000, we have the production capacity and export infrastructure to deliver consistently.

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?
Our MOQ varies by product type — typically starting from 200 pieces per style. For custom orders with specific branding, colours, or labels, the MOQ may differ. Please contact us directly with your product requirements and we'll provide a tailored quote.

What are your production timelines for bulk orders?
Production timelines vary depending on the quantity, customisation complexity, and current production schedule. We ensure timely delivery with consistent quality at every stage. Once your order is confirmed, we'll provide a precise timeline and keep you updated throughout production.

Do you offer custom designs?
Yes. We offer comprehensive customisation options for businesses: custom colours, chest logo printing, back branding, sleeve prints, custom tags, private labelling, and more. Share your artwork and requirements and our team will guide you through the process.

What payment terms do you offer for bulk orders?
Payment terms are flexible and tailored to the order size and our working relationship. Our team will walk you through the available options — including advance payment structures and milestone-based payments for larger orders. Reach out to discuss terms that work for both parties.

Do you handle international shipping for bulk orders?
Yes. We manage end-to-end international shipping with proper packaging, export documentation, and logistics coordination. We have an established export track record, particularly to South Africa, and are equipped to handle customs requirements for multiple international markets.

What about quality assurance for bulk production?
Quality is built into every stage of our process. We follow strict quality checks from fabric procurement through cutting, stitching, finishing, and final inspection — ensuring consistency in sizing, colour, and construction across every piece in your order. Our goal is zero surprises when your shipment arrives.
