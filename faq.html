Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know about our products, processes, and policies — answered clearly and directly.

Are your clothes good quality?
Yes. We use premium fabrics and conduct thorough quality checks on every product before shipping. Our manufacturing process is held to international export standards, ensuring that every piece you receive — from stitching to finishing — reflects the quality that Paanthera stands for.

What sizes do you offer?
We offer a full size range: S, M, L, XL, and XXL across all standard products. Custom sizes are also available on request for bulk orders. Please contact us with your specific size requirements and we'll advise accordingly.

Do you accept bulk or wholesale orders?
Yes — bulk and wholesale orders are our speciality. We work with wholesalers, sports clubs, retailers, and distributors across global markets. Whether you need 500 pieces or 20,000, we have the production capacity and export infrastructure to deliver consistently.

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?
Our MOQ varies by product type — typically starting from 500 pieces per style. For custom orders with specific branding, colours, or labels, the MOQ may differ. Please contact us directly with your product requirements and we'll provide a tailored quote.

What are your production timelines for bulk orders?
Production timelines vary depending on the quantity, customisation complexity, and current production schedule. We ensure timely delivery with consistent quality at every stage. Once your order is confirmed, we'll provide a precise timeline and keep you updated throughout production.

Do you offer custom designs?
Yes. We offer comprehensive customisation options for businesses: custom colours, chest logo printing, back branding, sleeve prints, custom tags, private labelling, and more. Share your artwork and requirements and our team will guide you through the process.

What payment terms do you offer for bulk orders?
Payment terms are flexible and tailored to the order size and our working relationship. Our team will walk you through the available options — including advance payment structures and milestone-based payments for larger orders. Reach out to discuss terms that work for both parties.

Do you handle international shipping?
Yes. We manage end-to-end international shipping with proper packaging, export documentation, and logistics coordination. We have an established export track record across global markets and are equipped to handle customs requirements for multiple international destinations.

What about quality assurance for bulk production?
Quality is built into every stage of our process. We follow strict quality checks from fabric procurement through cutting, stitching, finishing, and final inspection — ensuring consistency in sizing, colour, and construction across every piece in your order. Our goal is zero surprises when your shipment arrives.

Still Have Questions?

Get in touch directly — our team will respond with pricing, availability, and a tailored proposal for your requirements.
