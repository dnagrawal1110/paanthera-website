The Company

Blue Ocean Global

Blue Ocean Global is a premium apparel company delivering world-class garments defined by precision, craftsmanship, and elevated design. Our strength lies in refined execution, modern expertise, and a global vision built on uncompromising standards.

Operating under the Paanthera brand, we specialise in export-grade activewear — bridging India's world-class manufacturing capabilities with the growing demand for premium sportswear Globally and international wholesale markets.

From our first bulk order to African Countries, we built our reputation on one principle: every garment that leaves our facility must be something we're proud to put the Paanthera name on. Today, we export 25+ styles — all fully customisable and built to international standards.

Our Core Values
