The Company

Blue Ocean Global

Blue Ocean Global is a premium apparel company delivering world-class garments defined by precision, craftsmanship, and elevated design. Our strength lies in refined execution, modern expertise, and a global vision built on uncompromising standards.

Operating under the Paanthera brand, we specialise in export-grade activewear — bridging India's world-class manufacturing capabilities with the growing demand for premium sportswear in South Africa and international wholesale markets.

From our first bulk order to South Africa, we built our reputation on one principle: every garment that leaves our facility must be something we're proud to put the Paanthera name on. Today, we export a comprehensive range of 25+ polyester jerseys, cotton t-shirts, shorts, and trousers — all fully customisable and built to international standards.

"We don't just manufacture garments. We manufacture trust — stitch by stitch, order by order."
